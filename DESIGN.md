# Design

Night-only reader theme for *Dark Cloud* (moonlit village, dark genie, lavender "Dark Cloud" logo). Tailwind tokens in `tailwind.config.mjs` (indigo-ink `void`/`obsidian`, lavender-silver `slate` lights, `moon-*` gold ornaments, `lavender-*` links/active states, `violet-*` glows, Cinzel headings, Jost body, Courier Prime for the ASCII text) and component classes in `src/styles/global.css`. `color-scheme: dark`; there is no light mode or toggle.

- **Background:** `public/background.jpg` (night village) fixed behind a dark violet overlay gradient so text stays readable (`.site-bg` in `BaseLayout.astro` / `global.css`). On small or touch screens it is not fixed: `background-mobile.jpg` sits behind the top 75% of the page and fades into the plain page colour.
- **Branding:** `public/logo.png` (transparent PNG) in the sidebar, mobile header and home page; favicons are `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-512.png` and `apple-touch-icon.png`. Glows are violet/lavender.
- **Navigation:** solid indigo-ink sidebar (`.ink-sidebar`, no blur) with a gold edge and a faint tiled starfield (white, gold and lavender dots) under a violet top glow; collapsible, state in localStorage; mobile drawer shares the same list. Under the logo sits a gold scroll flourish either side of a crescent moon with a sparkle on each side (inline SVG in `SidebarNav.astro`). No diamonds anywhere in the sidebar.
- **Sidebar group cards (`.sidebar-card`, a `<details>`):** softly rounded (12px), translucent indigo, a fine gold outline plus a fainter inner hairline (`::before`). Closed = gold outline; hover (closed only, `@media (hover: hover)`) = lavender frame, violet fill and glow, violet band behind the title; open = brighter gold frame with a soft gold glow; the open card holding the current page (`:has([data-active])`) also gets a violet band behind its title. Group titles and page rows are Almendra SC (15px / 13px); group icons gold. The current page is a violet glow chip. Read pages show a larger gold tick (`a.block[data-page-id][data-done]`); the home page list and the footer medallion still use green. Tried and rejected during design: gold rods/scroll look, diamond studs, corner accents, chamfered game-menu frame, cloud fringe.
- **Page body:** `.page-title` in Almendra SC as silver-chrome lettering (violet outline and glow, banded silver face drawn from `data-title`, gold four-point sparkles; static, no motion) over a `.page-title-rule` of gold scroll flourishes either side of a crescent moon, dark ink `<pre>` with a thin gold frame, green "read" states.
- **Page top bar (`.crumb-bar`):** a quiet "etched ribbon" above the title, deliberately dimmer than the header: fading gold hairlines above and below a soft violet band, a small outlined Cinzel "Contents" chip with a gold crescent, a gold sparkle, then the section name. Breadcrumb only (Prev/Next live in the footer pager).
- **Page footer (`.pager`):** one row on every width — `◆‹ Previous )( ✓ )( Next ›◆`. Prev/next are indigo banners with a gold edge, pointed outer ends tipped by a gold diamond stud, and a curved inner notch hugging the round "mark as read" medallion (layers share a `clip-path` for the point and a radial `mask` for the notch; sizes are CSS variables on `.pager`). Hover: brighter gold edge, violet glow, one moonlight sheen sweep. The medallion is icon-only (tooltip + aria-label), muted gold when unread, emerald with a glow when read, with a small pop on toggle. On the first/last page the missing side is an empty slot so the medallion stays centred. Below 560px the banners hide the page titles (kept for screen readers). Motion is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Originals:** the unoptimised art lives in `raw-sources/art/` (gitignored); `public/` holds the compressed copies.
